Home Assistant: Turn On Hallway Lights on Motion (Auto-Off)

A copy-paste automation that turns on hallway lights when motion is detected after sunset, then turns them off after 5 minutes.

automation:
  - alias: Turn on hallway lights after sunset
    trigger:
      - platform: state
        entity_id: binary_sensor.hall_motion_sensor
        to: 'on'
    condition:
      - condition: sun
        after: sunset
    action:
      - service: light.turn_on
        entity_id: light.hall_lights
      - delay: 00:05:00
      - service: light.turn_off
        entity_id: light.hall_lights

How to adapt

  • Change binary_sensor.hall_motion_sensor to your motion sensor entity ID.
  • Change light.hall_lights to your smart light entity ID.
  • Adjust the delay duration for auto-off.
